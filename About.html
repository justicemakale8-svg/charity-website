CHARITY DESCRIPTION.

CHARITY:
is the voluntary act of giving help, money, food, or other support to people in need without expecting anything in return.

Examples:

  1. She donated money to charity to help homeless families
  2. Acts of charity including helping the poor, caring for the sick, and support those in difficulty.


NOTE:
The word charity also refers tokindness and generosity towards others.
